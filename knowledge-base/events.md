### ITC Welcome Day: New Members Orientation & Club Showcase
- **Event ID:** EVT-001
- **Category:** Networking / Club Gathering
- **Date & Time:** Sunday, November 15, 2026, from 10:00 to 16:00
- **Location / Venue:** Main Auditorium and Courtyard - Faculty of Sciences, Saad Dahleb University of Blida
- **Target Participants:** All university students (all faculties and years), new and existing ITC members, prospective volunteers
- **Capacity / Limits:** 250 attendees (open entry for the courtyard exhibition, seated registration for the auditorium sessions)
- **Registration Deadline:** November 13, 2026
- **Detailed Overview & Objectives:**
  - Introduce students to ITC, its mission, its departments (AI, Web Development, Design, Marketing, Automation, Media, Logistics), and the benefits of membership
  - Present the full 2026-2027 calendar of workshops, competitions, and conferences, including the flagship ITC Full-Stack & AI Challenge

  - Organize icebreaker activities, a tech quiz, and a networking lunch break so new members can meet their future teammates

- **Prizes & Rewards:** Free ITC membership card for every registered attendee, welcome kit (stickers, notebook, and printed workshop calendar), raffle prizes (tech accessories and course vouchers), and participation certificates on request
- **How to Register / Rules:** Register through the ITC Hub web app or the registration form shared on ITC social media pages. Bring your student card for check-in. Entry is free. Walk-in visitors may access the courtyard exhibition subject to available space.

### ITC Full-Stack & AI Challenge
- **Event ID:** EVT-002
- **Category:** Competition (Flagship Event)
- **Date & Time:** December 15-17, 2026, from 09:00 to 18:00 each day
- **Location / Venue:** Computer Labs 1-3 and Amphitheater A - Faculty of Sciences, Saad Dahleb University of Blida
- **Target Participants:** University students, junior developers, and AI enthusiasts from Saad Dahleb Blida University and other Algerian universities
- **Capacity / Limits:** 160 participants / 40 teams maximum
- **Registration Deadline:** December 10, 2026
- **Detailed Overview & Objectives:**
  - Flagship three-day competition where teams design, build, and present a complete web application that integrates at least one AI feature (for example a chatbot, a recommendation engine, a document assistant, or an automated classification system)
  - Day 1 covers the official kickoff, release of the challenge themes, team formation for solo registrants, and the start of development with mentor check-ins
  - Day 2 is dedicated to development with technical mentoring sessions on front-end, back-end, API design, database modeling, and prompt engineering
  - Day 3 includes the final code freeze, live demos of 5 minutes per team in front of the jury, and the awards ceremony
  - Evaluation criteria: innovation (20%), technical quality and architecture (30%), effective use of AI (20%), UI/UX design (15%), and presentation and business value (15%)
  - Two tracks: "Student Track" for beginners and second-year students, and "Open Track" for advanced participants
  - Objectives: encourage practical full-stack skills, promote responsible AI usage, and connect talented students with mentors and industry partners
- **Prizes & Rewards:** Cash prizes for the top three teams of the Open Track (1st place: 60,000 DZD, 2nd place: 40,000 DZD, 3rd place: 20,000 DZD), special prizes for the best UI/UX and best AI integration, cloud credits and learning vouchers from partners, certificates for all participants, and mentorship opportunities with industry experts
- **How to Register / Rules:** Register through the ITC Hub web app. Teams of 2 to 4 members; individuals may register alone and will be matched to a team. Each participant may join only one team. Projects must be started during the event; pre-built projects are disqualified, while open-source libraries and public APIs are allowed. All code must be pushed to a GitHub repository created for the challenge. Participants must bring their own laptops and chargers. The jury's decisions are final.

### ITC CodeSprint: Competitive Programming Contest
- **Event ID:** EVT-003
- **Category:** Competition / Competitive Programming
- **Date & Time:** Thursday, January 14, 2027, from 13:00 to 18:00
- **Location / Venue:** Computer Labs 1-3 - Computer Science Department, Saad Dahleb University of Blida
- **Target Participants:** Computer science and engineering students, algorithm and problem-solving enthusiasts (beginners are welcome in the Division 2 track)
- **Capacity / Limits:** 90 participants / 30 teams maximum
- **Registration Deadline:** January 7, 2027
- **Detailed Overview & Objectives:**
  - Five-hour team-based programming contest with 8 to 10 algorithmic problems of increasing difficulty, covering arrays, strings, sorting, recursion, greedy algorithms, graphs, and dynamic programming
  - Two divisions: Division 1 (advanced) and Division 2 (beginners and first-year students)
  - Live scoreboard managed on a contest judging platform, with a final freeze of the scoreboard during the last hour
  - A short preparation session is held one week before the event to explain the contest rules, submission format, and sample problems
  - Objectives: strengthen algorithmic thinking, prepare students for technical interviews and national programming contests, and identify talent for university representation in regional competitions
- **Prizes & Rewards:** Medals and trophies for the top three teams in each division, certificates for all participants, books and tech accessories for winners, and a priority invitation to the ITC competitive programming training group
- **How to Register / Rules:** Register through the ITC Hub web app. Teams of 3 members with one shared computer per team. Allowed languages: C, C++, Java, and Python. Internet access is limited to language documentation. Use of AI code assistants and communication with other teams are strictly prohibited and lead to disqualification. Ranking is based on the number of solved problems, then total penalty time.

### ITC TechTalks 2027: Annual Technology Conference
- **Event ID:** EVT-004
- **Category:** Conference
- **Date & Time:** Wednesday, April 7, 2027, from 09:00 to 17:00
- **Location / Venue:** Main Auditorium - Faculty of Sciences, Saad Dahleb University of Blida
- **Target Participants:** University students, teachers and researchers, startup founders, and technology professionals
- **Capacity / Limits:** 300 attendees
- **Registration Deadline:** March 31, 2027
- **Detailed Overview & Objectives:**
  - Annual one-day conference featuring keynote talks, short technical presentations, and panel discussions on current technology topics
  - Planned themes: Generative AI and agentic systems, cloud computing and DevOps, cybersecurity, product design, digital marketing and e-commerce in Algeria, and open-source contribution
  - Morning keynote by a guest speaker from the technology industry, followed by parallel sessions in the auditorium and Amphitheater B
  - Lightning talks of 10 minutes open to students and club members through a public call for speakers
  - A panel on "From Student to Professional: Building a Tech Career in Algeria" with entrepreneurs, engineers, and freelancers
  - Partner exhibition area where companies and startups present their products and open positions
  - Objectives: share knowledge beyond the classroom, encourage student speakers, and strengthen links between the university and the tech ecosystem
- **Prizes & Rewards:** Attendance certificates, conference swag bags, raffle prizes, and a "Best Lightning Talk" award for student speakers. Recordings and slides of approved sessions are shared with registered attendees after the event.
- **How to Register / Rules:** Register through the ITC Hub web app. Attendance is free, and seats are confirmed by email on a first-come, first-served basis. Speakers must submit a talk title and a short abstract through the call for speakers form before March 15, 2027. Attendees must show their registration QR code at the entrance.

### ITC HackBlida 2027: 24-Hour Hackathon
- **Event ID:** EVT-005
- **Category:** Hackathon
- **Date & Time:** Thursday, April 22 at 09:00 to Friday, April 23, 2027, at 09:00 (24 hours), with the closing ceremony on Friday at 11:00
- **Location / Venue:** Main Campus - Faculty of Sciences, Saad Dahleb University of Blida (Computer Labs and Amphitheater B)
- **Target Participants:** Students from all universities, developers, designers, and entrepreneurs
- **Capacity / Limits:** 120 participants / 30 teams maximum
- **Registration Deadline:** April 12, 2027
- **Detailed Overview & Objectives:**
  - Annual 24-hour hackathon where multidisciplinary teams build a working prototype that solves a real local problem
  - Announced tracks: Smart Education, E-Commerce and Social Commerce Automation, Health and Wellbeing, Sustainability and Smart City, and Open Innovation
  - Teams are encouraged to combine developers, designers, and business-minded members, and a team-formation session is held on the day before the event
  - Mentors from the ITC AI, Web, Design, and Marketing departments and invited experts are available throughout the night for technical and product guidance
  - Final pitches of 5 minutes plus 3 minutes of questions in front of a jury evaluating impact, technical execution, design, feasibility, and presentation
  - Meals, snacks, and drinks are provided during the event, and a rest area is available
  - Objectives: promote rapid prototyping, teamwork, and entrepreneurial thinking, and give the best projects a path toward incubation
- **Prizes & Rewards:** Cash prizes (1st place: 80,000 DZD, 2nd place: 50,000 DZD, 3rd place: 30,000 DZD), track awards for each theme, cloud credits and software licenses from partners, certificates for all participants, and incubation and mentorship opportunities for the strongest projects
- **How to Register / Rules:** Register through the ITC Hub web app by team or individually. Teams of 3 to 5 members. Code and design work must begin after the official start; existing ideas and pitch decks are allowed, but pre-built code is not. Teams must submit a repository link, a short demo video, and a one-page project summary before the deadline. Participants must follow the ITC Code of Conduct, and accommodation is not provided.

- **Location / Venue:** Conference Hall and Hall Exhibition Area - Saad Dahleb University of Blida
- **Target Participants:** Final-year students, graduates, ITC members, freelancers, startup founders, and recruiters
- **Capacity / Limits:** 150 attendees
- **Registration Deadline:** April 28, 2027

### ITC Graduation Projects Showcase
- **Event ID:** EVT-007
- **Category:** Exhibition / Showcase
- **Date & Time:** Sunday, June 6, 2027, from 09:30 to 16:30
- **Location / Venue:** Main Auditorium and Exhibition Hall - Faculty of Sciences, Saad Dahleb University of Blida
- **Target Participants:** Final-year and master's students presenting projects, supervisors, industry guests, and underclassmen
- **Capacity / Limits:** 40 project stands / 250 visitors
- **Registration Deadline:** Project submission deadline: May 23, 2027 (visitor registration closes June 3, 2027)
- **Detailed Overview & Objectives:**
  - Public exhibition where graduating students present their final-year projects (software applications, AI and data projects, embedded and IoT systems, cybersecurity tools, and design work) to peers, professors, and industry guests
  - Each project gets a stand with a demo, poster, and a 7-minute pitch session in the auditorium for shortlisted projects
  - Selected projects are reviewed by a mixed jury of professors and industry professionals considering originality, technical depth, real-world impact, and presentation quality
  - Side activity: mentoring corner where ITC members explain how to publish a project on GitHub, create a demo video, and convert a graduation project into a portfolio piece or startup idea
  - Objectives: celebrate student achievements, give visibility to quality work, and encourage the transformation of academic projects into products
- **Prizes & Rewards:** Awards for Best Overall Project, Best Innovation, Best AI Project, and Best Design, with trophies, certificates, and gifts from partners. All presenters receive a participation certificate, and outstanding projects are featured on ITC platforms.
- **How to Register / Rules:** Presenters submit their project through the ITC Hub web app, including a project summary (maximum 300 words), supervisor name, repository or demo link, and team members before the deadline. Selected projects are announced by email on May 30, 2027. Projects must be the participants' own work and approved by their academic supervisor. Visitors register through the ITC Hub web app and entry is free.
