### Artificial Intelligence (AI) Department
- **Department ID:** DEP-AI
- **Department Lead / Head:** ITC AI Team Lead - Head of the AI Department (appointed by the ITC executive board each academic year; the current name is published on ITC official channels)
- **Core Mission & Objectives:**
  - Make artificial intelligence accessible to all students by teaching its concepts, tools, and responsible use through hands-on practice rather than theory alone
  - Focus on three areas: generative AI and prompt engineering, agentic AI systems (agents with tools, memory, and planning), and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for building assistants that answer from private knowledge bases
  - Build practical AI solutions that solve local problems, such as customer support assistants, document question-answering tools, and intelligent recommendation features
  - Prepare members for the AI track of ITC competitions, including the ITC Full-Stack & AI Challenge and ITC HackBlida
  - Promote ethical AI practices: data privacy, bias awareness, and verification of AI-generated outputs
- **Key Technologies & Tools Used:** Python, Jupyter and Google Colab, Pandas and NumPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch, Hugging Face, OpenAI and Anthropic APIs, LangChain, vector databases (such as Chroma, Qdrant, and Pinecone), FastAPI, and Streamlit
- **Main Activities & Projects:**
  - Delivers the workshops "Building Agentic AI Systems: From LLMs to Autonomous Agents" (WS-001) and "Prompt Engineering & RAG: Building AI Assistants With Your Own Data" (WS-011)
  - Develops and maintains the ITC knowledge-base assistant that answers member questions about workshops, events, and departments
  - Runs weekly study circles on machine learning fundamentals, LLM applications, and AI paper reading
  - Prepares mentors and technical challenge themes for the AI track of ITC competitions
  - Builds team projects such as sentiment analysis tools, chatbots for small businesses, and image classification demos
- **How to Join & Member Requirements:** Submit the ITC membership application through the ITC Hub web app and select the AI Department as your first choice. Applicants complete a short entry task (for example, a notebook that cleans a small dataset or a simple chatbot that calls an LLM API) and attend a 15-minute motivation interview. Basic Python knowledge is recommended but not mandatory for the beginner learning path. Members are expected to attend weekly sessions and contribute to at least one department project per semester.
- **Target Audience / Who Should Join:** Computer science students, curious beginners with basic programming skills, and advanced members interested in machine learning, LLM applications, and AI product development

### Web & Mobile Development Department
- **Department ID:** DEP-DEV
- **Department Lead / Head:** ITC Web Development Team Lead - Head of the Web & Mobile Development Department (appointed by the ITC executive board each academic year; the current name is published on ITC official channels)
- **Core Mission & Objectives:**
  - Train members to design, build, and deploy modern web and mobile applications, from the first HTML page to production-ready full-stack products
  - Focus on front-end development, back-end APIs, databases, authentication, and cross-platform mobile apps
  - Maintain the technical products of the club, including the ITC Hub web app used for registrations and member management
  - Encourage good engineering habits: Git workflows, code reviews, testing, documentation, and clean architecture
  - Prepare teams for the ITC Full-Stack & AI Challenge and other development competitions
- **Key Technologies & Tools Used:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, Python (Django and FastAPI), PostgreSQL, MongoDB, REST APIs, Flutter, React Native, Git and GitHub, Visual Studio Code, Postman
- **Main Activities & Projects:**
  - Delivers the workshops "Web Development Foundations: Build and Deploy Your First Website" (WS-002) and "Git & GitHub Essentials: Version Control and Team Collaboration" (WS-003)
  - Develops and maintains the ITC Hub web app and the club's public website
  - Organizes internal mini-projects in small squads, where members build a complete application from planning to deployment over six to eight weeks
  - Holds code review sessions and pair-programming evenings where senior members mentor newcomers
  - Contributes to open-source projects and prepares members for the ITC Full-Stack & AI Challenge
- **How to Join & Member Requirements:** Apply through the ITC Hub web app and choose the Web & Mobile Development Department. Applicants complete an entry task, such as building a small responsive page or a simple to-do application, and publish it on GitHub. A short interview checks motivation and commitment. No advanced experience is required; beginners start in the foundations track and move to project squads after completing their first milestone.
- **Target Audience / Who Should Join:** Beginners who want to learn web development from scratch, intermediate developers looking for team projects, and students interested in building mobile applications

### Automation & Low-Code Department
- **Department ID:** DEP-AUTO
- **Department Lead / Head:** ITC Automation Team Lead - Head of the Automation & Low-Code Department (appointed by the ITC executive board each academic year; the current name is published on ITC official channels)
- **Core Mission & Objectives:**
  - Teach members how to automate repetitive business and academic processes using workflow automation and low-code platforms, with little or no traditional coding
  - Focus on workflow orchestration with n8n, API and webhook integrations, data synchronization between tools, and the integration of AI steps into automated flows
  - Build automation solutions for real use cases, such as order handling, customer support, appointment booking, and lead management for small businesses and e-commerce stores
  - Help members develop entrepreneurial skills by turning automation knowledge into services they can offer as freelancers or agency founders
  - Promote reliable automation design through error handling, testing, monitoring, and documentation
- **Key Technologies & Tools Used:** n8n, webhooks and REST APIs, JSON, Google Workspace (Sheets, Forms, Gmail, Calendar), Airtable, Notion, WhatsApp automation tools, Telegram bots, Zapier and Make (for comparison), Docker (for self-hosting n8n), and LLM APIs for AI-powered steps
- **Main Activities & Projects:**
  - Delivers the workshop "Workflow Automation with n8n: Connect Your Apps Without Heavy Coding" (WS-007)
  - Builds sample automations such as a form-to-spreadsheet pipeline with notifications, an AI-powered message classifier, and an order and reservation assistant over messaging platforms
  - Maintains a library of reusable workflow templates for club operations, including registration confirmations, reminders, and feedback collection
  - Collaborates with the AI Department on assistant projects and with the Digital Marketing Department on lead-generation and follow-up flows
  - Hosts "Automation Clinics", where members bring a real process and design the automation together
- **How to Join & Member Requirements:** Apply through the ITC Hub web app and select the Automation & Low-Code Department. The entry task is to build a simple workflow, for example an automation that receives a form response, saves it in a spreadsheet, and sends a confirmation email. Applicants then attend a short interview. A basic understanding of APIs and JSON is helpful but can be learned during onboarding. Members are expected to document each workflow they build.
- **Target Audience / Who Should Join:** Beginners and intermediate members, aspiring freelancers and entrepreneurs, business and management students, and developers who want to deliver solutions faster

### DevOps & Cloud Infrastructure Department
- **Department ID:** DEP-OPS
- **Department Lead / Head:** ITC DevOps Lead - Head of the DevOps & Cloud Infrastructure Department (appointed by the ITC executive board each academic year; the current name is published on ITC official channels)
- **Core Mission & Objectives:**
  - Provide members with practical knowledge of deploying, operating, and securing applications on modern infrastructure
  - Focus on Linux administration, containerization, cloud services, continuous integration and delivery (CI/CD), monitoring, and basic cybersecurity practices
  - Host and maintain the infrastructure of the club's digital products and provide a safe environment for members to deploy their projects
  - Prepare members for cloud certifications and DevOps careers
  - Build a culture of reliability, automation, and security awareness across all departments
- **Key Technologies & Tools Used:** Linux (Ubuntu), Bash, Docker and Docker Compose, Amazon Web Services (EC2, S3, IAM, RDS, Lambda), Nginx, GitHub Actions, Terraform (introductory), Prometheus and Grafana, SSH, and basic security tools
- **Main Activities & Projects:**
  - Delivers the workshops "Cloud Computing with AWS: Core Services and Hands-On Deployment" (WS-005), "Docker & Containerization: Ship Your Applications Anywhere" (WS-010), and supports the "Cybersecurity Fundamentals" workshop (WS-008)
  - Hosts and monitors the ITC Hub web app and other club services, including backups and uptime checks
  - Builds CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions for department projects
  - Organizes study groups for AWS Cloud Practitioner preparation
  - Provides deployment support to teams during ITC Full-Stack & AI Challenge and ITC HackBlida
- **How to Join & Member Requirements:** Apply through the ITC Hub web app and select the DevOps & Cloud Infrastructure Department. Applicants complete an entry task, such as installing a Linux virtual machine and deploying a simple containerized application, then attend an interview. Familiarity with the command line and basic networking is recommended. Members must follow security rules for credentials and cloud billing, and they are trained to use free tier resources responsibly.
- **Target Audience / Who Should Join:** Students interested in systems, networks, cloud computing, and DevOps careers, as well as developers who want to understand how their applications run in production

### UI/UX Design Department
- **Department ID:** DEP-UX
- **Department Lead / Head:** ITC Design Team Lead - Head of the UI/UX Design Department (appointed by the ITC executive board each academic year; the current name is published on ITC official channels)
- **Core Mission & Objectives:**
  - Develop members' skills in user interface design, user experience research, and visual identity so that club products and member projects are usable and visually consistent
  - Focus on user research, wireframing, prototyping, design systems, accessibility, and design handoff to developers
  - Maintain the visual identity of ITC, including its logo usage, color palette, typography, and templates for events and workshops
  - Support other departments by designing interfaces, posters, presentation decks, and social media visuals
  - Encourage collaboration between designers and developers through shared workflows
- **Key Technologies & Tools Used:** Figma and FigJam, Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, Canva, Adobe Express, Lottie animations, Notion, Miro, and accessibility checking tools
- **Main Activities & Projects:**
  - Delivers the workshop "UI/UX Design with Figma: From Wireframe to Interactive Prototype" (WS-006)
  - Designs and maintains the interface and design system of the ITC Hub web app
  - Creates visual materials for events such as the ITC Full-Stack & AI Challenge, ITC HackBlida, and ITC TechTalks, including posters, badges, certificates, and slide templates
  - Runs weekly design challenges (for example redesigning a known app screen) with peer feedback sessions
  - Conducts usability tests of club products with real users and shares improvement reports with the Web & Mobile Development Department
- **How to Join & Member Requirements:** Apply through the ITC Hub web app and choose the UI/UX Design Department. Applicants submit a small portfolio or complete a design task, such as redesigning a landing page or a mobile screen in Figma, and attend a short interview. No prior professional experience is required. Members are expected to present their work in design critique sessions and to contribute to at least one club design request each month.
- **Target Audience / Who Should Join:** Creative thinkers, aspiring designers, developers who want better design skills, and students of any faculty interested in visual communication

### Digital Marketing & Content Department
- **Department ID:** DEP-MKT
- **Department Lead / Head:** ITC Marketing Lead - Head of the Digital Marketing & Content Department (appointed by the ITC executive board each academic year; the current name is published on ITC official channels)
- **Core Mission & Objectives:**
  - Promote ITC activities and grow the club's community on social media and other channels
  - Teach members digital marketing fundamentals, including content strategy, copywriting, social media management, paid advertising, analytics, and personal branding
  - Produce the content that represents ITC: announcements, recaps, event coverage, photos, short videos, newsletters, and blog posts
  - Prepare members to work as marketers, content creators, community managers, or freelancers
  - Collaborate with sponsors and partners by creating clear presentations and promotional packages
- **Key Technologies & Tools Used:** Meta Business Suite, Meta Ads Manager, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Telegram, Canva, CapCut, Google Analytics, Google Sheets, Mailchimp, and ChatGPT and other AI tools for content support
- **Main Activities & Projects:**
  - Supports the workshops "Meta Ads & Digital Marketing for Small Businesses" (WS-004) and "Personal Branding & Freelancing for Tech Students" (WS-012)
  - Manages the official ITC social media pages and the content calendar for workshops, competitions, and conferences
  - Runs promotional campaigns for events, including organic content and small paid advertising tests
  - Produces photo and video coverage of events and publishes recaps and speaker highlights
  - Prepares the ITC newsletter and the sponsorship deck used for partner outreach
- **How to Join & Member Requirements:** Apply through the ITC Hub web app and select the Digital Marketing & Content Department. Applicants complete a content task, such as writing a short event announcement, designing a social media post, or editing a 30-second video, and attend an interview. Good writing skills in Arabic, French, or English and a regular presence on social platforms are valuable. Members must respect the ITC brand guidelines and the approval process before publishing.
- **Target Audience / Who Should Join:** Students interested in marketing, communication, content creation, and entrepreneurship, from any faculty and with no technical background required

### Logistics & Events Management Department
- **Department ID:** DEP-LOG
- **Department Lead / Head:** ITC Logistics & Events Lead - Head of the Logistics & Events Management Department (appointed by the ITC executive board each academic year; the current name is published on ITC official channels)
- **Core Mission & Objectives:**
  - Plan, organize, and run all ITC workshops, competitions, conferences, and social gatherings smoothly and professionally
  - Manage practical aspects of each event, including venue booking, equipment, schedules, registration check-in, catering, safety, and attendee support
  - Coordinate with university administration, faculty representatives, partners, and guest speakers
  - Train members in project management, teamwork, negotiation, and public communication
  - Ensure each event follows the ITC Code of Conduct and collects feedback for continuous improvement
- **Key Technologies & Tools Used:** Notion, Trello, Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Forms, Calendar), Microsoft Excel, QR code check-in tools, ITC Hub web app (registration dashboard), WhatsApp and Telegram groups for coordination, and Canva for printed materials
- **Main Activities & Projects:**
  - Organizes the full schedule of workshops, from room reservation to equipment testing and attendance tracking
  - Leads the operations of flagship events such as ITC Welcome Day, ITC Full-Stack & AI Challenge, ITC CodeSprint, ITC TechTalks, ITC HackBlida, ITC Career & Networking Night, and the ITC Graduation Projects Showcase
  - Prepares event budgets, sponsor requirements, volunteer rosters, and run-of-show documents
  - Manages check-in teams, badges, certificates, and the post-event feedback survey
  - Holds a debrief meeting after each event to document lessons learned
- **How to Join & Member Requirements:** Apply through the ITC Hub web app and choose the Logistics & Events Management Department. Applicants complete a planning task, such as drafting a one-page plan for a small workshop with a timeline, needs list, and responsibilities, and attend an interview. Reliability, punctuality, and communication skills matter most, and no technical background is required. New members first volunteer at one event as part of the onboarding and then take on a defined responsibility.
- **Target Audience / Who Should Join:** Organized and proactive students from any faculty, natural leaders, people who enjoy teamwork and problem solving, and members who want experience in event management
